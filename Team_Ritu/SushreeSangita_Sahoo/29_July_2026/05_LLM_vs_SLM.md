# LLM vs SLM

## Large Language Model (LLM)

LLMs are AI models trained on massive datasets with billions of parameters. They provide high-quality responses and can perform many complex tasks.

### Advantages

- Strong reasoning
- Better accuracy
- Supports complex tasks

## Small Language Model (SLM)

SLMs are smaller AI models with fewer parameters. They require less memory and are faster on local devices.

### Advantages

- Faster inference
- Lower hardware requirements
- Suitable for edge devices

## Comparison

| LLM                      | SLM                         |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Large model size         | Small model size            |
| Higher accuracy          | Faster execution            |
| More hardware needed     | Runs on lightweight devices |
| Better for complex tasks | Better for simple tasks     |

## Conclusion

LLMs provide better performance, while SLMs are ideal when speed, cost, and efficiency are important.
